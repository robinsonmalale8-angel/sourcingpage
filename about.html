About Us

Founded in 2023 by Robinson Larence Malale and Elay Maria Peter, Ultimate Sourcing was
created with a clear mission - to simplify and improve the sourcing process for
businesses and individuals across Tanzania. Based in Kariakoo Masasi, Dar es Salaam, we are connected
with trusted and certified suppliers from China while ensuring a smooth,
reliable, and transparent sourcing experience from start to finish.

At Ultimate Sourcing, we specialize in sourcing goods from China to Dar es Salaam,
offering complete support in order management, shipping and logistics, sample and
quality inspection, as well as customs and clearing assistance. We understand the common
challenges businesses face, including delayed shipments, unreliable suppliers, poor
product quality, and long waiting times. That is why our team focuses on efficiency, trust,
and quality control to ensure every order meets the expectations of our clients.

Our vision is to become Tanzania’s number one sourcing agent by helping businesses
grow through dependable international trade solutions. Whether you are a small business
owner, retailer, wholesaler, or individual buyer, Ultimate Sourcing is committed to
delivering professional service, timely communication, and reliable sourcing solutions that
save you time, reduce risks, and help your business succeed.
